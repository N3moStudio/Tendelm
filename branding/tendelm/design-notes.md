# Tendelm 01

Brief: quiet, low-effort habits and time away; ivory botanical silhouette on forest green. Retain the calm inherited UI palette. No score pressure, visual noise, or extra onboarding.

Working name: Tendelm (tend + elm; TEND-elm). Private sideload identity. Preliminary public exact-name searches on 2026-11-04 found incidental text/OCR matches, not a relevant app; this is not a clearance result. Commercial trademark, domain registration, store-name reservation and native-speaker checks were not commissioned or completed. Morroway, Daymere and Avenlo were discarded after direct software-brand collisions. Sources: https://www.morroway.app/ ; https://daymere.com/ ; https://avenlo.app/ .

No baked outer rounded-square mask. Identity changes and project application were explicitly requested by the user. Flattened catalog route used to preserve generated artwork without computer control; no hand-authored Icon Composer file or claimed Composer preview.

Prompt: original iOS icon for calm habit/time-since app Tendelm; full opaque forest-green (#294D43) square; one warm ivory elm-seed/frond with two broad asymmetrical curved lobes and a short bent stem; satin ceramic relief, no tiny veins or decoration, no letters, clocks, calendars, checkmarks, border, mockup or outer rounded mask. Bold silhouette at small sizes.
